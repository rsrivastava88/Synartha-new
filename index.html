Questions, answered honestly.

Is this a coding course?

No. This is a Tech Sales, Business Development and Lead Generation fellowship. No programming required.

Do I need previous sales experience?

No. The fellowship starts from the fundamentals and takes you to execution over 12 weeks.

Who can apply?

Final-year students, fresh graduates, job seekers, career switchers and aspiring freelancers.

What tools will I learn?

LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Apollo, Clay, Lusha, Upwork, CRM, email outreach and AI tools.

Is AI included?

Yes. AI accelerates research, personalization, qualification and sales workflows, with human judgment always in charge.

Will I build a portfolio?

Yes, that's the core. Campaigns, prospect lists, proposals and case studies you can show.

How long is the fellowship?

12 weeks, split into four phases: understand, master tools, execute, build your career.

What happens after the fellowship?

You'll have a sales portfolio, interview prep and a job-search strategy. Eligible participants may be introduced to relevant hiring opportunities through the employer network where applicable.

Are jobs guaranteed?

We don't make guarantees of employment or income. We build the skills, proof and positioning that make you competitive. Outcomes depend on you and the market.
